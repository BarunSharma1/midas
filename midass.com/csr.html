CSR Programs

CSR-Led Skill Development Programs

MIDAS Skills undertakes and implements placement-linked skill development training projects as per partner requirements in their CSR obligation in the area of education, employment, and livelihood enhancement.

These programs are specifically targeted at underprivileged youth and women, empowering them with vocational skills and creating pathways to sustainable livelihoods.

Focus Areas

Our CSR Project Areas

Youth Empowerment

Skills training and vocational education for underprivileged youth to help them gain employability and pursue sustainable careers.

Women Empowerment

Specialized skill development programs for women focused on self-reliance, entrepreneurship, and financial independence.

Entrepreneurship Development

Training candidates who wish to start their own businesses through our Entrepreneurship Development Program (EDP).

Student Empowerment

Skill development training for school students, exposing them early to vocational skills and career pathways.
