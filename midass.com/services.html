Service 01

Apprenticeship Advisory & Services

As one of the leading Third-Party Aggregators (TPA) authorized under NAPS and NATS, we provide comprehensive apprenticeship solutions to organizations across sectors.

Our services include apprenticeship program design, candidate sourcing and onboarding, compliance management, stipend processing, and government liaison for seamless execution of apprenticeship programs.

Work Integrated Learning Programs (WILP)

Our WILP programs bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements, providing students and fresh graduates with real-world work experience alongside their academic curriculum.

We partner with leading educational institutions and corporations to design and deliver programs that combine classroom learning with on-the-job training, creating a pipeline of skilled, job-ready professionals.

Industry Partnerships
