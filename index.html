About Us

The bridge between raw talent and real industry demand

MIDAS Skills is a Pune-based workforce solutions company committed to one mission — making every young Indian employable. We combine government-backed apprenticeship frameworks with industry-designed curricula to create talent pipelines that are skilled, certified and productive from day one.

As a registered NAPS & NATS facilitator, we manage the entire apprenticeship lifecycle for our clients — sourcing, documentation, compliance, stipends and mentoring — while our staffing and upskilling verticals keep workforces agile as markets change.
